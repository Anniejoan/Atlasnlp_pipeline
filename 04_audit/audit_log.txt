Prompt v2.2 frozen after development pilot. 
On a fixed 50-record development sample from the Stage-1 audit, v2.2 completed 50/50 records with 100% precision among explicit country assertions and no false explicit assignments on reference-empty records. 
Remaining discrepancies were primarily conservative abstentions, partial country-set recovery, or intentional exclusion of reuse-only records. 
Because further tuning risked trading precision for recall, v2.2 was frozen for population-wide re-extraction.


Population-wide v2.2 re-extraction completed. Of 18,035 original Core candidate records, 18,031 were successfully processed.

Note that EXCLUDED_AMBIGUOUS in the merge report (90) doesn't equal MULTIPLE_DATASETS_AMBIGUOUS relationship count (42) — a 48-record difference. That's not a bug in this script; it's pass-through of an eligibility flag computed upstream in content_country_v2.py (ambiguous country evidence, not just ambiguous dataset match, also excludes a record from primary_eligible), and this script correctly just consumes that flag rather than recomputing it.
Country normalization QC table (24 rows) is sensible — England/Scotland/Wales→UK, Hong Kong→China, Puerto Rico→US, etc., all consistent with the stated "prior AtlasNLP ontology" convention; ambiguous "Congo" and aggregate "Worldwide"/"Multiple countries" tokens are correctly dropped rather than guessed; explicit evidence correctly outranks inferred when they collapse to the same normalized country.
